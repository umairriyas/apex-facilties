Our Story

Built on Hard Work & a Commitment to Excellence

Apex Facilities was founded in 2010 with a simple belief — that every business and property owner deserves a facilities management partner they can truly rely on. We started small, with a single team and a handful of local clients in Las Vegas, NV.

Over the years, we have grown our team, expanded our service offering and built lasting relationships with clients across healthcare, education, corporate, retail and residential sectors. Through it all, our core commitment has never changed: deliver consistent, professional results that make our clients' facilities cleaner, safer and better maintained.

Today, Apex Facilities is one of Las Vegas's most trusted facilities management companies — and we take that responsibility seriously on every job, every day.
